experience

Research and industry experience, teaching, awards, activities, and education.

Research Experience

CoVA: Compiler for Versatile Architectures (Ph.D. Thesis)
Jun. 2023 – Present, CORSA Lab, UC Irvine
Sole developer of an MLIR-based compiler that maps NumPy programs to CPUs and GPUs: a custom MLIR dialect and 30 passes lowering to six CPU/GPU code-generation routes, analyses that keep buffers GPU-resident, deterministic GPU reductions, and a PyTorch path for persistent model executables. The project was initiated during the 2023 HPE internship.

HeteroTask: Distributed Heterogeneous Task Runtime
Jan. 2025 – Apr. 2026, CORSA Lab, UC Irvine
Sole developer of a DAG execution framework with task-variant profiling, memory-aware device and variant selection, communication-cost modeling, and buffer lifetime planning, plus a C++/MPI runtime that executes the planned placements across CPU/GPU nodes. Its scheduling concepts later informed CoVA.

PyAIE: A Python-based Programming Framework for Versal ACAP
Sep. 2022 – Jun. 2023, CORSA Lab, UC Irvine
Led the design and implementation of a Python-based framework that generates AI Engine kernels, programmable-logic and host code, and configuration scripts for AMD Versal ACAP.

Industry Experience

Hewlett Packard Enterprise - Research Associate Intern
Jun. 2024 – Sep. 2024, Hewlett Packard Labs, Milpitas, CA · HeteroBench
Lead developer of HeteroBench, a benchmark suite of 11 multi-kernel applications with Python, OpenMP, CUDA, and HLS implementations for CPUs, GPUs, and FPGAs. Built a JSON-configured Python driver with Jinja-generated Makefiles for backend-specific builds, benchmark execution, profiler integration, and experiment logging. The work received the ICPE 2025 Best Paper Award - Artifact Track.

Hewlett Packard Enterprise - Research Associate Intern
Jun. 2023 – Sep. 2023, Hewlett Packard Labs, Milpitas, CA · CoVA
Built CoVA’s initial compilation framework: a custom MLIR dialect separating program semantics from target-specific lowering, MLIR-to-LLVM lowering, Python bindings, and compiler analysis and transformation passes. This work led to a U.S. patent application with Hewlett Packard Enterprise (US 2025/0370738 A1), now allowed by the USPTO.

IMBED LLC - Development Engineer Trainee
Sep. 2021 – Jan. 2022
Designed components for a real-time JPEG2000 player for digital cinema applications, with emphasis on FPGA implementation of the MQ entropy decoder.
