**Sep. 2022 - Jun. 2023**

Led the design and implementation of a Python-based framework that generates AI Engine kernels (with AIE vector intrinsics), programmable-logic and host code, and configuration scripts for AMD Versal ACAP.

Links: [DAC 2023 Late Breaking Results](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10247843), [LATTE 2023 paper](https://capra.cs.cornell.edu/latte23/paper/10.pdf), [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QrtP7Fri4pI).
